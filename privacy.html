Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

WordHive Puzzles does not collect or sell any personal information, and sends nothing to me or to any server. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics services, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Everything WordHive Puzzles saves stays under your control:

  • Your settings — accent colours, appearance, sound and voice preferences, and your multiplayer name — are stored locally on the device.
  • Your puzzles — games in progress, finished puzzles, the puzzles you create or import (their letters, words, and titles), and the word list you are building in Create — are stored on the device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud key-value storage so they can continue on your other devices. This data lives in your Apple Account. I have no access to it, and Apple's privacy policy governs it.
  • Photos of imported puzzles are kept on the device that read them, so the puzzle can be corrected later. They are never uploaded or synced, and they are deleted along with the puzzle.

Deleting the app removes its local settings and imported photos. Saved puzzles live in iCloud, so they outlast the app. At any time, Settings → Reset all progress deletes your games in progress and finished puzzles, and Settings → Delete all My Puzzles deletes the puzzles you created or imported, on every device.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected — no name, email, contacts, or location — and photos you import never leave your device.
  • No identifiers are used for tracking, and no data is shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No usage analytics are collected or uploaded. The app ships with analytics collection disabled at the source level.
  • Puzzles are generated on your device or bundled with the app, so playing on your own needs no network access.

Camera and photos

To import a printed word search, WordHive Puzzles uses the camera on iPhone and iPad, or the photos and image files you choose. It sees only the photos you take or pick, and reads them entirely on your device.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer connects directly to other devices on the same Wi-Fi network or close by, with no server in between. The app asks for Local Network access to find and join games. A game shares your player name — your device's name until you type another — along with your finds and score with the other players in it, and a host's name is visible to anyone nearby looking for games. Multiplayer games are not saved.

Speech

Spoken celebration phrases use Apple's built-in speech synthesis with the voices installed on your device. WordHive Puzzles does not record audio and does not use the microphone. Dictating words in Create on Apple TV uses the system's own dictation.

Children

WordHive Puzzles is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of WordHive Puzzles collects anything, I will update this policy before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email me at wordhivepuzzlesapp+support@gmail.com.
