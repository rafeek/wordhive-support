WordHive

Privacy policy — last updated 28 August 2026

WordHive does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics services, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Everything WordHive saves stays under your control:

  • Your settings — accent colors, appearance, sound and voice preferences — are stored locally on the device.
  • Your game in progress is stored on the device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud key-value storage so it can continue on your other devices. This data lives in your Apple Account. I have no access to it, and Apple's privacy policy governs it.

Deleting the app removes its local data. To clear the synced game, delete the app from each device and remove its iCloud data in your device settings.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected — no name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
  • No identifiers are used for tracking, and no data is shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No usage analytics are collected or uploaded. The app ships with analytics collection disabled at the source level.
  • Puzzles are generated on your device, so the app requires no network access to play.

Speech

Spoken celebration phrases use Apple's built-in speech synthesis with the voices installed on your device. WordHive does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Children

WordHive is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of WordHive collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
