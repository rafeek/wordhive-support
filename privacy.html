Privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

WordHive Puzzles does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics services, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Everything WordHive Puzzles saves stays under your control:

  • Your settings — accent colors, appearance, sound and voice preferences — are stored locally on the device.
  • Your game in progress is stored on the device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud key-value storage so it can continue on your other devices. This data lives in your Apple Account. I have no access to it, and Apple's privacy policy governs it.

Deleting the app removes its local settings. Saved puzzles live in iCloud, so they outlast the app; Settings → Reset all progress deletes all of them, on every device, at any time.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected — no name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
  • No identifiers are used for tracking, and no data is shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No usage analytics are collected or uploaded. The app ships with analytics collection disabled at the source level.
  • Puzzles are generated on your device or bundled with the app, so the app requires no network access to play.

Speech

Spoken celebration phrases use Apple's built-in speech synthesis with the voices installed on your device. WordHive Puzzles does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Children

WordHive Puzzles is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of WordHive Puzzles collects anything, I will update this policy before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact
