WordHive Puzzles

Support & help

WordHive Puzzles is a word search game for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV. Pick a theme, get a puzzle built around it, and find every word.

Need a hand?
Email wordhivepuzzlesapp+support@gmail.com. Please include your device, your OS version, and the app version (shown in Settings) so I can reproduce the problem.

Frequently asked

How do I select a word?

Drag from the first letter to the last. On Apple TV, move the cursor with the remote, press to start a selection, and press again on the final letter. Press and hold play/pause to reveal the solution.

Where do the puzzles come from?

Every puzzle is generated on your device from the theme you type. Nothing is downloaded, so the app works fully offline.

Does my game carry over between devices?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud on each device. The game in progress syncs through your private iCloud account. If a device is offline, it will catch up the next time it connects.

My game did not sync.

Check that iCloud Drive is on and that all devices use the same Apple Account. Open the app while connected and give it a moment; syncing happens when the app comes to the foreground.

How do I change the voice or turn off sound?

Open Settings inside the app. You can toggle sound effects and spoken celebrations independently, and pick from the voices installed on your device.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Settings lets you set separate accent colors for light and dark appearance, choose a background style, and force light or dark mode.

The app never suggests the word I wanted.

Themes are matched against a built-in word bank. Try a broader theme (“ocean” rather than “tide pools of Maine”). Suggestions for missing themes are welcome by email.

Does WordHive Puzzles collect any data about me?

No. See the privacy policy.
