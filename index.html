Support

Help and contact for WordHive Puzzles

How to play

Controls differ by device, so each platform has its own guide: iPhone & iPad, Mac, and Apple TV. The same guide is in the app under Settings → How to Play, or Help → WordHive Help (⌘?) on the Mac.

Frequently asked

Where do the puzzles come from?

Every puzzle is drawn from a word bank bundled with the app. Some boards are built on your device and some are pre-built and shipped with the app; nothing is downloaded, so the app works fully offline. There are 18 themes, each drawing on several subcategories, so the same theme gives different boards over time.

Does my game carry over between devices?

Yes, when each device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on. Your game in progress is saved as you play and synced when the app comes to the foreground. A device that is offline catches up the next time it connects.

My game did not sync.

Check that all devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for the app. Bring the app to the foreground on the device that last played, wait a moment, then open it on the other device. Very recent moves can take a minute to arrive.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad — iOS / iPadOS 17 or later
  • Mac — macOS 14 or later
  • Apple TV — tvOS 17 or later
