How to play on Apple TV

The same guide is in the app under Settings → How to Play

The goal

#
Find every hidden word

Each puzzle hides a list of themed words in a grid of letters. Find them all to finish the puzzle — the honeycomb bar at the top fills as you go.

Finding words

#
Aim with the remote

Swipe on the remote's touch surface to glide the cursor across the grid. Click the first letter, then the last letter, to select a word. Correct words lock in and cross off the word list.

Restart a selection

Press play/pause to clear a selection you've started and aim again.

Play with a game controller

Point the left stick where you want the cursor: centred is the middle of the board, pushed all the way is its edge, and letting go leaves the cursor where it is. The d-pad steps one letter at a time. A selects a letter, X clears a selection, and B goes back.

Steering on hex grids

On Hex and Twist grids, swipe diagonally to step to a diagonal neighbour — the swipe snaps to the nearest of the six directions.

Word directions

Words run in straight lines — forwards, backwards, or diagonally. Square grids use 8 directions, and hex grids use 6.

Twist grids

On a Twist grid each word bends through neighbouring cells, often folded back on itself and crossing other words. Click its letters one after another, from either end. The few spare letters are the puzzle's subtheme, scrambled.

Hear a word

Swipe right past the edge of the grid to move into the word list, then click a word to hear it spoken. Swipe left past the first column to return to the grid.

Moving the grid

#
Board size

The whole board stays on screen, so there's nothing to zoom or pan — just aim the cursor with the remote.

Scoring

#
Word values

Each word starts at a high value that ticks down the longer it takes to find. The faster you spot it, the more points it adds to your score.

Combos

Finding words back-to-back keeps your streak alive for bonus points. Pauses break the chain, but bigger grids give you longer between finds.

Setting up a puzzle

#
Size, shape & level

Choose a grid size (Small, Medium, Large), a shape (Square, Hex or Twist), and a word level — Simple for everyday words, Advanced adds harder ones. Highlight a bar and swipe left or right, or click to step through its options. Then pick a theme.

Theme badges

A tick means you finished that theme with the current size, shape, and level, and a play triangle marks a puzzle in progress.

Hold to choose

Press and hold the remote's click on a started or finished theme to continue it, start a new puzzle, or play it again. It also lists any puzzle for that theme left in progress under a different size, shape or level, so you can jump back into it.

Create a puzzle

#
1
Start a new puzzle

Open My Puzzles on the menu and choose Create.

2
Name it and pick a shape

Type a title, then choose Square, Hex or Twist.

3
Choose a size

Leave Auto size on to get the smallest board that holds every word, or turn it off and choose the size yourself.

4
Add your words

Add your words one at a time. Type them, or hold the Siri button to dictate. As you go, the board is tried in the background and you're told how many words fit and how many letters are filler.

5
Preview the board

Select Preview to see the board beside your words, with any left out and a bigger size suggested. Solution shows or hides where the words are, and Try Again makes another board.

6
Save and play

Save keeps the puzzle in My Puzzles, and Play starts it.

Change the list

Undo takes back the last change, and Clear empties the list. The list is kept in iCloud until you clear it or save or play it as a puzzle.

My puzzles

#
Play them again

Your puzzles are saved and synced like the themes. They're listed in My Puzzles: select one to continue it, or use the buttons beside it to preview its board and words, edit it or delete it.

Edit a puzzle

Editing a puzzle you made lets you rename it, change its words, shape or size, and regenerate the board. Editing an imported puzzle (on iPhone, iPad or Mac) opens it in Import again to correct it, with the photos it was read from on the device that imported it. Photos aren't synced.

Delete them all

Settings → Delete all My Puzzles removes every one of them, on every device.

Multiplayer

#
1
Host a game

Choose Multiplayer on the menu, type the name the others will see, and choose Host a Game. Everyone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network or nearby, and up to eight can play.

2
Join a game

Choose Multiplayer: games hosted nearby are listed there, each with its own Join button. You can join until the host presses Start.

3
Pick the puzzle

While the others join, pick a theme or one of My Puzzles from the menu. A theme gets a fresh board made just for this game. Stop Hosting cancels the game.

4
Start together

The board stays hidden until everyone starts. The host presses Start first, which closes the game to newcomers. Everyone else then has 5 seconds to press Start, or the count starts over.

Play together

Everyone plays the same board. Each word can be found only once: whoever finds it first gets the points, and it's crossed off for everyone. Scores are shown along the bottom, and the highest score wins.

Saving & resuming

#
Saving and syncing

Your current game is saved automatically and synced across your devices through iCloud. Select Continue on the menu to jump back in.

Reset all progress

Settings → Reset all progress deletes every game in progress and every finished puzzle, on every device. My Puzzles are kept, back at the start.

Settings

#
Open Settings

Select Settings on the menu.

Sound

Turn sound effects and spoken words on or off, and pick a voice.

Appearance

Switch between light and dark, turn the aurora background on or off, choose the menu-to-game transition, and turn on the OLED screen saver.

Accessibility

#
VoiceOver

Words in the list read as found or not found, and every word you find is announced. The grid itself is played by aiming the cursor with the remote.

Reduce Motion

When Reduce Motion is on in Settings → Accessibility → Motion, the moving background, the menu's bees and wavy title, menu transitions, and found-word flourishes (confetti, bounces, flying points) are switched off.
