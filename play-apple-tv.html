How to play on Apple TV

The same guide is in the app under Settings → How to Play

The goal

Find every hidden word

Each puzzle hides a list of themed words in a grid of letters. Find them all to finish the puzzle — the honeycomb bar at the top fills as you go.

Finding words

Aim with the remote

Swipe on the remote's touch surface to glide the cursor across the grid. Click the first letter, then the last letter, to select a word. Correct words lock in and cross off the word list.

Restart a selection

Press play/pause to clear a selection you've started and aim again.

Play with a game controller

Point the left stick where you want the cursor: centred is the middle of the board, pushed all the way is its edge, and letting go leaves the cursor where it is. The d-pad steps one letter at a time. A selects a letter, X clears a selection, and B goes back.

Steering on hex grids

On Hex and Twist grids, swipe diagonally to step to a diagonal neighbour — the swipe snaps to the nearest of the six directions.

Any straight line

Words run in straight lines — forwards, backwards, or diagonally. Square grids use 8 directions; hex grids use 6.

Twist grids wind

On a Twist grid each word bends through neighbouring cells, often folded back on itself and crossing other words. Click its letters one after another, from either end. The few spare letters are the puzzle's subtheme, scrambled.

Hear a word

Swipe right past the edge of the grid to move into the word list, then click a word to hear it spoken. Swipe left past the first column to return to the grid.

Moving the grid

It always fits

The whole board stays on screen, so there's nothing to zoom or pan — just aim the cursor with the remote.

Scoring

Be quick

Each word starts at a high value that ticks down the longer it takes to find. The faster you spot it, the more points it adds to your score.

Build a combo

Finding words back-to-back keeps your streak alive for bonus points. Pauses break the chain, but bigger grids give you longer between finds.

Setting up a puzzle

Size, shape & level

Choose a grid size (Small, Medium, Large), a shape (Square, Hex or Twist), and a word level — Simple for everyday words, Advanced adds harder ones. Highlight a bar and swipe left or right, or click to step through its options. Then pick a theme.

Theme badges

A tick means you finished that theme with the current size, shape, and level; a count (e.g. 3/12) marks a puzzle in progress.

Hold to choose

Press and hold the remote's click on a started or finished theme to continue it, start a new puzzle, or play it again. It also lists any puzzle for that theme left in progress under a different size, shape or level, so you can jump back into it.

My puzzles

Create your own

Open My Puzzles on the menu, choose Create, and pick a shape. Auto size finds the smallest board that holds every word and shows its size; turn it off to choose the size yourself. Add your words one at a time — type them, or hold the Siri button to dictate; Undo takes back the last change to the list. The list is kept in iCloud until you clear it. As you edit, the puzzle is tried in the background and you're told how many words fit and how many letters are filler. Select Preview to see the board beside your words, with any left out and a bigger size suggested; select Solution to show or hide where the words are. Save keeps the puzzle in My Puzzles, and Play starts it.

Play them again

Your puzzles are saved and synced like the themes. They're listed in My Puzzles: select one to continue it, or use the buttons beside it to edit it or delete it. Editing a puzzle you made lets you rename it, change its words, shape or size, and regenerate the board. Editing an imported puzzle (on iPhone, iPad or Mac) opens it in Import again, with the photos it was read from, to correct it.

Multiplayer

Host a game

Choose Multiplayer on the menu and Host a Game, then wait for the others to join — everyone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network or nearby. Pick a theme (a fresh board is made just for this game) or one of My Puzzles, then press Start when everyone's in — players can keep joining until then, and your Start is also your ready.

Join a game

Choose Multiplayer: games hosted nearby are listed there, each with its own Join button. You can join until the host presses Start.

Play together

Everyone plays the same board. It stays hidden until the host presses Start and everyone else presses Start within 5 seconds of the host and each other — if anyone takes longer, the count starts over. Each word can only be found once: whoever traces it first gets the points, and it's crossed off for everyone. Scores are shown along the bottom, and the highest score wins.

Saving & resuming

Picks up where you left off

Your current game is saved automatically and synced across your devices through iCloud. Select Continue on the menu to jump back in. Settings → Reset all progress deletes every saved puzzle, on every device.

Make it yours

Sound & looks

Open Settings from the gear on the menu to toggle sound effects and spoken words, pick a voice, switch between light and dark, set your accent colours, choose the menu-to-game transition, and turn on the OLED screen saver.

Accessibility

VoiceOver

Words in the list read as found or not found, and every word you find is announced. The grid itself is played by aiming the cursor with the remote.

Reduce Motion

When Reduce Motion is on in Settings → Accessibility → Motion, the moving background, menu transitions, and found-word flourishes are switched off.
