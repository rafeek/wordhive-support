How to play on iPhone & iPad

The same guide is in the app under Settings → How to Play

The goal

#
Find every hidden word

Each puzzle hides a list of themed words in a grid of letters. Find them all to finish the puzzle — the honeycomb bar at the top fills as you go.

Finding words

#
Tap or drag a line

Drag your finger from the first letter to the last, or tap the first then the last letter. Correct words lock in and cross off the word list.

Selecting & scrolling

Settings → Touch chooses what one finger does. Drag selects, and two fingers scroll. Tap picks words by their first and last letter, and one finger scrolls. Auto drags to select, but once you've tapped a first letter one finger scrolls until you tap the last.

Word directions

Words run in straight lines — forwards, backwards, or diagonally. Square grids use 8 directions, and hex grids use 6.

Twist grids

On a Twist grid each word bends through neighbouring cells, often folded back on itself and crossing other words. Trace its letters one after another, from either end. The few spare letters are the puzzle's subtheme, scrambled.

Double-tap a word

Double-tap any word in the list to see its dictionary definition.

Moving the grid

#
Zoom and pan

Pinch to zoom into a large grid and drag with two fingers to pan — or one finger, under the Tap and Auto touch settings. Pinch back out to see the whole board.

Scoring

#
Word values

Each word starts at a high value that ticks down the longer it takes to find. The faster you spot it, the more points it adds to your score.

Combos

Finding words back-to-back keeps your streak alive for bonus points. Pauses break the chain, but bigger grids give you longer between finds.

Setting up a puzzle

#
Size, shape & level

Pick a grid size (Small, Medium, Large), a shape (Square, Hex or Twist), and a word level — Simple for everyday words, Advanced adds harder ones. Then choose a theme.

Theme badges

A tick means you finished that theme with the current size, shape, and level, and a play triangle marks a puzzle in progress.

Touch and hold a theme

Touch and hold a started or finished theme to continue it, start a new puzzle, or play it again. It also lists any puzzle for that theme left in progress under a different size, shape or level, so you can jump back into it.

Create a puzzle

#
1
Start a new puzzle

Open My Puzzles on the menu and choose Create.

2
Name it and pick a shape

Type a title, then choose Square, Hex or Twist.

3
Choose a size

Leave Auto size on to get the smallest board that holds every word, or turn it off and choose the size yourself.

4
Add your words

Add your words one at a time, or paste a whole list. As you go, the board is tried in the background and you're told how many words fit and how many letters are filler.

5
Preview the board

Preview shows the board, with any words left out and a bigger size suggested. Holding Solution shows where the words are, and Try Again makes another board.

6
Save and play

Save keeps the puzzle in My Puzzles, Cancel leaves without it, and Play starts it.

Change the list

Undo takes back the last change, Clear empties the list, and Copy List puts it on the clipboard. The list is kept in iCloud until you clear it or save or play it as a puzzle.

Import a printed puzzle

#
1
Start an import

Open My Puzzles on the menu and choose Import.

2
Add the photos

Take a photo of a printed word search, or choose photos. The grid and its word list can be in one photo or one each, in any order or way up.

3
Check the letters

The grid and word list are read for you. A letter misread in a way only one fix explains is corrected automatically, and Undo puts it back. Likely misreads are offered under Possible fixes.

4
Fix the rest

Tap a letter to change it, or to delete a stray row or column. Tap a word to correct it, delete it or see where it was read in the photo.

5
Check the words

Each word says whether it was found in the grid. One read apart from the rest of the list, often part of a heading, is marked to check. Words not found in the grid are left out when you play.

6
Title and save

The printed name is used as the title when it can be told. Any word that isn't in the grid, often part of the puzzle's heading, can be made the title instead. Save keeps the puzzle in My Puzzles, and Cancel leaves without it.

Add a missing photo

A photo of the word list can be added after the grid, and a retaken grid replaces the first one.

My puzzles

#
Play them again

Your puzzles are saved and synced like the themes. They're listed in My Puzzles: tap one to continue it, tap the eye beside it to preview its board and words, or swipe it left to edit or delete it.

Edit a puzzle

Editing a puzzle you made lets you rename it, change its words, shape or size, and regenerate the board. Editing an imported puzzle (on iPhone, iPad or Mac) opens it in Import again to correct it, with the photos it was read from on the device that imported it. Photos aren't synced.

Delete them all

Settings → Delete all My Puzzles removes every one of them, on every device.

Printing

#
1
Print a puzzle

Choose Print on the menu, then pick a theme or one of My Puzzles. A theme prints with the size, shape and level chosen on the menu: the puzzle you have in progress if there is one, otherwise a new board. Regenerate makes another.

2
Set up the page

The preview shows the page as it will print. Choose Portrait or Landscape and the paper size. For a puzzle you've started, choose whether to print it clean or with the words you've found marked. Print sends it to a printer, and Done leaves Print.

Multiplayer

#
1
Host a game

Choose Multiplayer on the menu, type the name the others will see, and choose Host a Game. Everyone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network or nearby, and up to eight can play.

2
Join a game

Choose Multiplayer: games hosted nearby are listed there, each with its own Join button. You can join until the host presses Start.

3
Pick the puzzle

While the others join, pick a theme or one of My Puzzles from the menu. A theme gets a fresh board made just for this game. Stop Hosting cancels the game.

4
Start together

The board stays hidden until everyone starts. The host presses Start first, which closes the game to newcomers. Everyone else then has 5 seconds to press Start, or the count starts over.

Play together

Everyone plays the same board. Each word can be found only once: whoever finds it first gets the points, and it's crossed off for everyone. Scores are shown along the bottom, and the highest score wins.

Saving & resuming

#
Saving and syncing

Your current game is saved automatically and synced across your devices through iCloud. Tap Continue on the menu to jump back in.

Reset all progress

Settings → Reset all progress deletes every game in progress and every finished puzzle, on every device. My Puzzles are kept, back at the start.

Settings

#
Open Settings

Tap Settings on the menu.

Sound

Turn sound effects and spoken words on or off, and pick a voice.

Appearance

Switch between light and dark, set your accent colours, turn the aurora background on or off, and choose the menu-to-game transition.

Touch

Choose whether one finger selects letters or scrolls the board, as described under Finding words.

Accessibility

#
VoiceOver on the grid

Each letter reads as its row, column, and letter, and says whether it is selected or found. Double-tap the first letter of a word, then its last (on a Twist grid, each of its letters in turn), to select it. The Clear selection action drops a selection you've started.

VoiceOver in the word list

Words in the list read as found or not found. Double-tap one to hear it, or use the Define action. Every word you find is announced.

Reduce Motion

When Reduce Motion is on in Settings → Accessibility → Motion, the moving background, the menu's bees and wavy title, menu transitions, and found-word flourishes (confetti, bounces, flying points) are switched off.

Hear a word

Tap any word in the list to hear it spoken in the voice chosen in Settings.
