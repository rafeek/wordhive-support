How to play on Mac

The same guide is in the app under Help → WordHive Help (⌘?)

The goal

Find every hidden word

Each puzzle hides a list of themed words in a grid of letters. Find them all to finish the puzzle — the honeycomb bar at the top fills as you go.

Finding words

Click or drag a line

Drag from the first letter to the last, or click the first then the last letter. Correct words lock in and cross off the word list.

Any straight line

Words run in straight lines — forwards, backwards, or diagonally. Square grids use 8 directions; hex grids use 6.

Twist grids wind

On a Twist grid each word bends through neighbouring cells, often folded back on itself and crossing other words. Trace its letters one after another, from either end. The few spare letters are the puzzle's subtheme, scrambled.

Double-click a word

Double-click any word in the list to open its definition in the Look Up panel.

Moving the grid

Zoom and pan

Pinch on the trackpad to zoom into a large grid and scroll to pan. Pinch back out to see the whole board.

Scoring

Be quick

Each word starts at a high value that ticks down the longer it takes to find. The faster you spot it, the more points it adds to your score.

Build a combo

Finding words back-to-back keeps your streak alive for bonus points. Pauses break the chain, but bigger grids give you longer between finds.

Setting up a puzzle

Size, shape & level

Pick a grid size (Small, Medium, Large), a shape (Square, Hex or Twist), and a word level — Simple for everyday words, Advanced adds harder ones. Then choose a theme.

Theme badges

A tick means you finished that theme with the current size, shape, and level; a count (e.g. 3/12) marks a puzzle in progress.

Right-click a theme

Right-click (or click and hold) a started or finished theme to continue it, start a new puzzle, or play it again. It also lists any puzzle for that theme left in progress under a different size, shape or level, so you can jump back into it.

My puzzles

Create your own

Open My Puzzles on the menu, choose Create, and pick a shape. Auto size finds the smallest board that holds every word and shows its size; turn it off to choose the size yourself. Add your words one at a time or paste a whole list; Copy List puts it back on the clipboard, Clear empties it, and Undo takes back the last change to the list. The list is kept in iCloud until you clear it. As you edit, the puzzle is tried in the background and you're told how many words fit and how many letters are filler. Preview shows the board, with any words left out and a bigger size suggested; hold Solution to see where the words are. Save keeps the puzzle in My Puzzles, and Play starts it.

Import a printed puzzle

In My Puzzles, choose Import and pick photos or image files of a printed word search — the grid and its word list in one, or one of each, in any order or way up. Its letter grid and word list are read for you; a letter misread in a way only one fix explains is corrected automatically, and you can click any letter or word to fix the rest — or click a letter to delete a stray row or column. Clicking a word shows where it was read in the photo; a word that isn't in the grid (often part of the puzzle's heading) can be made the title. The printed name is used as the title when it can be told. Save keeps the puzzle in My Puzzles; Cancel leaves without it.

Play them again

Your puzzles are saved and synced like the themes. They're listed in My Puzzles: click one to continue it, or use the buttons beside it to edit it or delete it. Editing a puzzle you made lets you rename it, change its words, shape or size, and regenerate the board. Editing an imported puzzle (on iPhone, iPad or Mac) opens it in Import again, with the photos it was read from, to correct it.

Multiplayer

Host a game

Choose Multiplayer on the menu and Host a Game, then wait for the others to join — everyone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network or nearby. Pick a theme (a fresh board is made just for this game) or one of My Puzzles, then press Start when everyone's in — players can keep joining until then, and your Start is also your ready.

Join a game

Choose Multiplayer, then Join a Game, and pick the host from the list. You can join until the host presses Start.

Play together

Everyone plays the same board. It stays hidden until the host presses Start and everyone else presses Start within 5 seconds of the host and each other — if anyone takes longer, the count starts over. Each word can only be found once: whoever traces it first gets the points, and it's crossed off for everyone. Scores are shown along the bottom, and the highest score wins.

Saving & resuming

Picks up where you left off

Your current game is saved automatically and synced across your devices through iCloud. Click Continue on the menu to jump back in. Settings → Reset all progress deletes every saved puzzle, on every device.

Make it yours

Sound & looks

Open Settings (⌘,) to toggle sound effects and spoken words, pick a voice, switch between light and dark, set your accent colours, and choose the menu-to-game transition.

Accessibility

VoiceOver

Each letter reads as its row, column, and letter, and says whether it is selected or found. Activate the first letter of a word, then its last, to select it; the Clear selection action drops a selection you've started. Words in the list read as found or not found — activate one to hear it, or use the Define action. Every word you find is announced.

Reduce Motion

When Reduce Motion is on in System Settings → Accessibility → Display, the moving background, menu transitions, and found-word flourishes are switched off.

Hear a word

Click any word in the list to hear it spoken in the voice chosen in Settings.
